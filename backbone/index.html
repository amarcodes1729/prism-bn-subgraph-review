Use continuous scores from 0 to 1 for the five criteria. The separate text score is from 1 to 5.

      

        Scoring guide — keep this open while reviewing
        

          
NodesHow completely does the generated text cover the distinct concepts, entities, and variables in the original?

          
StatesAre the original values, categories, dates, quantities, and alternatives represented under the right concepts?

          
EdgesAre the original connections, directions, dependencies, and relationships represented without reversals or inventions?

          
Relationship typeDoes the generated text preserve whether a relationship is causal, historical, administrative, conditional, temporal, or merely associated?

          
Overall similarityHow faithfully does the generated text preserve the original meaning, scope, important facts, and qualifiers?

          
Direct text scoreGive one independent overall rating for the original versus generated text, from 1 to 5.

        

        
Scale: 0.0 = no meaningful coverage · 0.5 = partial or mixed coverage · 1.0 = complete faithful coverage. For the direct score, 1 = very poor match, 3 = mixed match, and 5 = highly faithful match. Decimals are allowed.
