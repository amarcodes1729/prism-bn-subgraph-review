Technology ethics, also known as technoethics, is a field of ethical inquiry concerned with the questions and implications associated with technology. It applies ethical thinking to technologies as they are developed and studies the ethical implications that emerge as new technologies rise in prominence.

Technological development can generate ethical questions, growing concerns, and dilemmas for both producers and consumers. These issues help define the focus of technoethics, which examines how technology affects the people who create it as well as those who use it. Philosophers associated with the development of this field include Hans Jonas and Mario Bunge.
