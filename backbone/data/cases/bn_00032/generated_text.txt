A prominent advocate for ethical technology use has criticized the effects of modern technology on everyday life and society, warning in particular about the dangers information technology and large-scale platform amplification can pose to modern society. This work has been associated with the Center for Humane Technology, the Earth Species Project, Mozilla Labs, Twitter, and Apple.

These concerns have been communicated through the podcast *Your Undivided Attention*, an article written with Renée DiResta, and public quotations. A central distinction is between freedom of speech and “freedom of reach”: the ability to speak does not necessarily imply a right to unrestricted platform amplification. The phrase has helped frame discussion of the free-speech implications of limiting how widely content is distributed.

The idea spread through its initial coining, subsequent writing, and wider public use. It was publicly quoted by Sacha Baron Cohen and also used by Jack Dorsey. In the context of platform governance, the concept became connected with limiting reach and with explanations of the rationale for banning political advertisements.
