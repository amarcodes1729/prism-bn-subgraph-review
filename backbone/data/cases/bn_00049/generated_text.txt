The activist movement campaigns on behalf of poor and working people, seeking greater rights for those constituencies and an end to poverty. Its objectives are closely tied to the social conditions of poverty and homelessness, while its organizational outlook combines anti-poverty campaigning with militant activism and a left-wing anarchist orientation.

Its protests have targeted the closure of a low-income housing project as well as the 2010 Winter Olympics. In pursuing its campaign objectives, the movement has used sit-ins, squats, vandalism and other disruptive protests, including small-scale riots. The tactics employed have varied with both the target and the organization’s militant orientation: actions surrounding the housing-project closure were more associated with sit-ins and squats, while protests aimed at the Winter Olympics were more often disruptive and sometimes developed into small-scale riots.

These confrontational methods attracted considerable attention. Sit-ins, squats and vandalism often drew a public response, while disruptive protests and small-scale riots were especially likely to receive substantial notice.
