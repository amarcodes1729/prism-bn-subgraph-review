Tug of war is a contest in which two opposing teams pull on opposite ends of a rope, each seeking to overcome the other team’s pull. The rope may carry a centre-line marking and two markers positioned at equal distances from it. Depending on the rules, the objective is to pull the opposing marker across the centre line or to move the rope a specified distance in one direction.

Team size can vary, although teams may consist of eight or more members. Success depends on both the physical strength of the participants and their ability to cooperate as a team. These configurations are used across nation-team and club-team competitions, as well as indoor and outdoor contests.

International competition is governed by the Tug of War International Federation, which organizes World Championships, while club-team competitions form another part of the organized sport. Competition rules prohibit techniques such as touching the ground for an extended period or lowering an elbow below the knee during the pull.
