Assessments of casualties vary according to the population under consideration, including military personnel, civilians, and migrants. Immense casualties are the most prominent assessment for military personnel and civilians, with probabilities of about 60 percent in each case. For migrants, lower casualty estimates are more common than higher estimates, although no specific severity assessment remains the largest category.

Demographic uncertainty also shapes casualty assessments. An unknown death toll is associated most strongly with an assessment of immense casualties, at roughly 53 percent. When estimates diverge widely or determining the population size proves frustrating, lower and higher casualty estimates become more prominent, reflecting the difficulty of establishing the scale of losses.
